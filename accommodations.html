About This Layout

As you scroll down, you will notice that the background image stays locked tightly in place. This white container acts like a card layered over the top, moving independently up the screen.

Why Use This?

This approach creates a strong sense of depth and visual polish. It is highly effective for landing pages, portfolio websites, digital storytelling, or modern blog designs where you want a stunning cover photo to anchor the experience.

Customization Tips

You can easily adjust the width of this white container by changing the `max-width` property in the CSS. Removing the `max-width` entirely will make the white background cover the full width of the screen, creating a clean full-bleed card layout.
